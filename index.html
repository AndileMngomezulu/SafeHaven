💜 Safety guidance

  • If you are in immediate danger, seek emergency assistance and move somewhere safer if you can do so without increasing risk.
  • Consider contacting someone you trust and agreeing on a code word or check-in plan.
  • If your device may be monitored, use a safer device when possible. Private browsing does not guarantee that activity is hidden from every person or service.
  • Keep evidence only if it is safe to do so. Do not risk your safety to collect it.
  • When supporting someone, listen without judgement, respect their choices and help them access professional services if they want that support.

🔐 Your privacy matters

SafeHaven is currently a demonstration prototype. It has no account system, secure database, encrypted evidence storage, or automatic emergency alert service.

Contact entries exist only while the page remains open. Incident notes are previewed on the page and are not intentionally stored by this code.

GitHub Pages serves this website publicly. Do not submit real survivor information through this prototype.
